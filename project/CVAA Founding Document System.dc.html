Positioning
VAACA's mandate is CEMAC-wide from the outset — Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon — built by standing up one working national chapter first and replicating it, not by declaring a regional body before any chapter operates.
Phase 1 — Cameroon founding chapter
Charter, Readiness Framework and Coalition ratified; nine founding seats filled; declaration signed at the CBA Institutional Conference, Yaoundé.
Phase 2 — the other five CEMAC states
Cameroon's Charter, governance floor and PSAN Readiness Framework packaged as a template chapter for CAR, Chad, Congo, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon.
Phase 3 — CEMAC federation
Chapters federate under the Virtual Assets Association of Central Africa once two or more national coalitions are independently operating.
